One image, made to make the AI judge excited: a colossal glowing whale drifts over a neon city at dusk — and glowing inside its translucent belly is a whole tiny city of its own, streets and towers lit up like a world within a world. Scale is the surprise: the whale is the sky, the city is the whale.

The program is deliberately small: `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) checks that `entry.png` is a real PNG or JPEG under 1 MiB and no more than 1024 px on a side, then copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
